# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

KatLearn is a learning project intended to support students, teachers, and contributors. We are committed to making participation welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, appearance, background, identity, experience, or level of knowledge.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, constructive, and considerate.
- Give feedback focused on ideas and work, not personal attacks.
- Accept reasonable corrections and differing viewpoints.
- Respect other people's privacy; do not publish private information, credentials, or personal details without permission.
- Help keep discussions focused on improving KatLearn and its learning community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions, submitting malicious content, or encouraging others to do so.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a learning and collaboration space.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project-related communication, and other spaces where someone is representing KatLearn or its community.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile: https://github.com/ptnnmusicgroup-png. Please do not publish sensitive personal information in public issues. Reports will be reviewed fairly and as confidentially as reasonably possible.

The project maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action in response to violations. The maintainer will consider the seriousness and context of each incident.
